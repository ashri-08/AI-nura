Time Management & Environment

Managing your time and creating a suitable study environment can help you stay organised, focused and productive.

Pomodoro Technique

What is it? The Pomodoro Technique divides study time into focused periods followed by short breaks.

How is it helpful? It can make long study sessions feel more manageable and gives you regular opportunities to rest.

How to use it: Study for 25 minutes without distractions, take a 5-minute break, and repeat. After several rounds, take a longer break.

Structured Routine

What is it? A structured routine is a planned schedule that organises when and what you will study.

How is it helpful? Having a routine can help you manage your time and reduce the need to decide what to study every time you sit down.

How to use it: Create a realistic schedule that includes specific subjects, study periods and breaks. Try to follow the routine consistently.

Practice Papers

What is it? Practice papers are questions or tests completed before an actual assessment to practise applying your knowledge.

How is it helpful? They can help you become familiar with question styles, practise working under time limits and identify topics that need more revision.

How to use it: Complete questions without looking at your notes, check your answers afterwards, and use your mistakes to decide what you should revise.
